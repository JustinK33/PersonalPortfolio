About Me

I'm a Computer and Data Science student at Rutgers, focused on backend development and distributed systems. During my internship I built and shipped backend features end to end, which is where I started paying attention to what breaks at scale and why. There's a real gap between code that works and code that holds up under real conditions, and that gap is what I find interesting now.

I'm mostly interested in fault tolerance, consistency, and system design: how data stays correct when things go wrong, how systems degrade instead of collapsing, and how you plan for load you can't fully predict ahead of time.

Outside of school and code, I box, play basketball, volleyball, and pickleball, and pick up the guitar when I want to slow down. I'm also someone who needs regular walks with music in my ears to think clearly, and I make time for my friends whenever I can. It keeps me balanced against the hours I spend heads down in a terminal.
